01 / A little about me

Builder first.
Always learning.

I didn't begin my career with a blueprint for enterprise AI. I began by figuring out how data actually works.

My career has taken me through different sides of the same problem: getting information into the hands of people who need to make decisions. I've written the code, designed the platforms, worked with business teams, and led the people responsible for making it all work.

Retail has been a defining part of that journey. At Macy's and Levi Strauss & Co., I saw how data meets the real world — customers, stores, products, operations, and decisions that cannot wait for a perfect dashboard.

Today, as a Senior Solutions Architect at Databricks, I bring those experiences into conversations about modern data platforms and AI. The questions have grown bigger, but the instinct is the same: understand the problem, challenge assumptions, and build something useful.

03 / What experience taught me

“The most valuable data architecture is the one that changes how people work.”

I've seen organizations invest heavily in platforms while struggling to agree on what their numbers mean. AI raises the stakes: a model can generate an answer quickly, but the enterprise still needs shared context, trust, and human judgment.

That's the territory I write about now — the space between what technology makes possible and what organizations are ready to use.

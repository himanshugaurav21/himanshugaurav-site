Data has never been just about technology. It's about the experiences we create — and what becomes possible when humans, AI, and data work together.

Why I started writing

More than another technology publication.

Towards Data Experience began with a simple observation: organizations spend enormous energy moving, storing, and processing data, but the experience of using it often remains fragmented.

The publication is where I examine that gap. Sometimes through technical architecture. Sometimes through questions about organizational context, semantics, and AI. Always with the same aim: make the conversation about what data enables, not only the tools that power it.

The editorial lens

Three recurring questions.

What should data feel like?

Interfaces, workflows, and experiences that make intelligence accessible to more people.

What does AI need to know?

Business meaning, relationships, governance, and the context behind a credible answer.

What should we build differently?

Open architectures and operating models that can evolve without losing trust.

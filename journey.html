Markdown·approach.md
HOW I WORK

Understand first. Build for adoption.

A solution has to fit the people and organization expected to use it. I bring business and technical teams together around five questions.

  1. 01

    Understand

    What matters to the business? How do people work today?

  2. 02

    Align

    Who needs to agree, own the outcome, and lead the change?

  3. 03

    Design

    What should we build, buy, or simplify to fit this organization?

  4. 04

    Enable

    What will help people trust, adopt, and sustain the solution?

  5. 05

    Learn

    Is it being used? What is improving, and what needs to change?

People, process, and technology are part of the same design decision.

Markdown·defining-experiences.md
EXPERIENCES THAT SHAPED ME

Three moments. A broader perspective.

CHOICE THROUGH UNCERTAINTY

Keeping options open.

Leadership had not finalized the cloud platform. Open data architecture allowed me to move across three clouds as the direction evolved.

What stayed with me: architecture should give an organization room to change.

INSIGHTS AT THE POINT OF USE

Meeting the business where it works.

Starting with business needs, I proposed, deployed, and scaled a custom insights app globally, bringing information directly to users. It was an uncommon approach in the industry as I experienced it then.

What stayed with me: the experience of using information is part of its value.

FROM IDEAS TO PRACTICE

Making new approaches useful.

I enabled data products and data mesh while the ideas were still emerging. The work connected technical delivery with how teams organize around data.

What stayed with me: new ideas need ownership and ways of working to become useful.

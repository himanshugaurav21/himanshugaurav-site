Semantic Layers & Enterprise Ontology

A consistent business vocabulary connects analytics, applications, and AI agents to the same trusted enterprise meaning.

An evolving perspective

Beyond the technology.

One definition, many consumers

A metric such as net revenue should mean the same thing in a dashboard, SQL query, application, or AI answer. Shared definitions reduce reconciliation work.

Connect meaning to reality

The enterprise is a network of customers, products, locations, events, policies, and relationships. Semantic models and ontologies make parts of that context explicit.

Treat meaning as testable

Validate joins, grain, business calculations, access control, and cross-consumer consistency. Trust is built through repeatable checks, not just documentation.

Enterprise AI Architecture

Building trustworthy enterprise AI requires business context, evaluation, governance, and workflows that connect answers to decisions.

An evolving perspective

Beyond the technology.

Begin with the decision

A useful enterprise AI project begins with a decision or workflow, not a model selection. Who needs an answer, what will they do with it, and what would a wrong answer cost?

Make context available

Enterprise knowledge is fragmented across systems, documents, definitions, and people. AI agents need the right context, permissions, and evidence to produce answers people can trust.

Evaluate the whole system

Test factual accuracy, authorization, latency, cost, and how the AI experience supports actual decisions. A compelling demo is not proof of a dependable production system.
